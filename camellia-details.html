The Last Camellia


Author: Sarah Jio
Year: 2013
Genre: Historical mystery


Core plot overview:
1940 (Flora Lewis): On the brink of World War II, Flora - an American botanist - is blackmailed by an international flower thief into infiltrating Livingston Manor in England. Her secret mission is to find and steal the Middlebury Pink, the last surviving specimen of a rare camellia. Posing as a nanny for Lord Livingston's children, she grows attached to the family and discovers dark mysteries surrounding the death of the children's mother.
2000 (Addison Sinclair): Decades later, Addison, a landscape architect fleeing a troubling past in New York, moves into Livingston Manor with her husband after his family purchases the estate. Exploring the overgrown, abandoned camellia orchard, she uncovers clues left behind from 1940 and realizes that the secrets tied to the Middlebury Pink - and the danger behind them - are still very much alive.
