The Whisperer


Author: Donato Carrisi
Year: 2009
Genre: Novel, psychological thriller, crime fiction, mystery


Core plot overview:
The Whisperer (Italian title: Il suggeritore) follows a criminal investigation led by criminologist Goran Gavila and detective Mila Vasquez.
The investigation begins when investigators discover the remains of six missing girls, but there is no trace of a seventh girl. The discovery suggests that a highly intelligent serial killer may be responsible.
Mila, who specializes in finding missing children, joins Gavila's investigative team. As they examine the evidence, they uncover a complex pattern of crimes and realize that the killer may be manipulating other people into committing terrible acts.
The investigation becomes increasingly disturbing as the detectives discover unexpected connections between the victims, the suspects, and the killer. The case challenges their understanding of human behavior and forces them to confront the darkness hidden beneath ordinary appearances.
The novel combines psychological suspense, unexpected revelations, and a complex investigation into the nature of evil.
