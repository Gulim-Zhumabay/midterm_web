The Great Gatsby


Author: F. Scott Fitzgerald
Year: 1925
Genre: Novel, tragedy, modernist literature, social novel


Core plot overview:
The novel tells the story of Jay Gatsby, a wealthy and mysterious man who lives in a large mansion and hosts extravagant parties. Despite his wealth and popularity, Gatsby is unhappy because he is still in love with Daisy Buchanan, a woman he met and loved five years earlier.
The story is narrated by Nick Carraway, Gatsby’s neighbor and Daisy’s cousin. Nick helps Gatsby reunite with Daisy, who is now married to Tom Buchanan, a rich but unfaithful man.
Gatsby hopes to recreate his past relationship with Daisy, but their reunion leads to jealousy, conflict, and tragedy. After a car accident involving Daisy, Gatsby takes responsibility to protect her. Later, George Wilson, whose wife Myrtle was killed in the accident, believes Gatsby was responsible. He kills Gatsby and then himself.
In the end, Nick becomes disappointed with the wealthy society around him and realizes that money cannot bring true happiness or restore the past.
