Dark Psychology and Manipulation


Author: James Scott
Year: 2024
Genre: Non-Fiction, applied psychology, self-help, social psychology


Core plot overview:
This book is nonfiction, so it has no story. The author gives many real examples and scientific studies. He shows how dark psychology is used in personal relationships, company politics, and political fights. He also explains how to resist manipulation, protect yourself from aggressive influence, and stay mentally strong. The publisher says it contains 77 dark psychology techniques and ways to defend against them. The topics include deception, gaslighting, and Machiavellianism, and the cover also lists abuse and blackmail.
