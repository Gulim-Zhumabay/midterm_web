Dieu voyage toujours incognito


Author: Laurent Gounelle
Year: 2010
Genre: Mix of novel, psychology and philosophy.


Core plot overview:
Alan Greenmor stands on the Eiffel Tower, ready to end his life. A stranger in a dark suit, Yves Dubreuil, stops him. Dubreuil offers a deal: Alan gives his life over to him, and Dubreuil teaches him the secrets of happiness and success.
Alan starts a journey of self-discovery. He has hard and embarrassing moments, such as a humiliating fiasco at a Parisian bakery. He also finds the strength to speak up in his company’s boardroom. He learns to overcome his fears and self-doubt, take risks, and accept himself.
Later, Alan starts to doubt what Dubreuil really wants. He wonders who Dubreuil really is and who the mysterious people around him are. The book asks one big question: how can we go beyond our fears and habits, and leave a life path that does not make us happy?

Main Characters

  • Alan Greenmor
  • Yves Dubreuil
