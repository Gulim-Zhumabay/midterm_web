The Way of the Wolf


Author: Jordan Belfort
Year: 2017
Genre: Nonfiction, business, sales and persuasion, self-help.


Core plot overview:
This book has no story. Belfort gives a step-by-step system for selling and persuading people. He says he used this system to build wealth for himself, his clients, and his sales teams. The system is called “Straight Line Selling”. The seller controls the talk and moves the buyer from the start to the sale along the shortest path, a straight line. The seller stays on topic and does not waste time on subjects that have nothing to do with the sale.
